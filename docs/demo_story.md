# Demo Story

DisasterGuard supports a simple operator workflow:

1. Existing systems or analysts provide normalized hazard, exposure, urgency, and confidence inputs for a geolocated area.
2. DisasterGuard combines those inputs using a transparent weighted formula.
3. The API returns a priority score, severity, recommended action, risk drivers, and operator summary.
4. Operators can use the result to decide which area deserves earlier review, revisit, alerting, or response coordination.

## Wildfire / Haze Example

In Chiang Mai, an area may show a recent hotspot cluster, vegetation stress, low humidity, wind spread potential, and nearby community exposure. DisasterGuard ranks the area as high priority for operator review and local response coordination.

## Rapid Flood Example

In Chiang Rai, heavy rainfall accumulation, rapid rainfall intensity increase, river proximity, low-lying terrain, and bridge exposure may combine into a high priority result.

## Landslide Example

In Mae Hong Son, heavy rainfall, steep slope, soil moisture proxy, land-cover change, and transport route exposure may justify review in the next operational cycle.

DisasterGuard does not replace monitoring. It prioritizes attention.
